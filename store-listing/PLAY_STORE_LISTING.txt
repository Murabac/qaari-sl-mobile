FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) — copy below:
----------------------------------------------------------
Xulka Quraada brings you a curated archive of Quranic recitation from the Somali community — homeland and diaspora.

Discover reciters, browse surahs, and listen with a clean player built for focused listening. Save favorites and organize playlists so your most-loved recitations are always close at hand.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse approved reciters and their recitations
• Play any surah with clear next / previous controls
• Favorite recitations you want to return to
• Build personal playlists
• Create an account to sync favorites and playlists across devices

WHO IT’S FOR
Listeners who want authentic Somali Quranic voices in one place — whether you are learning, reflecting, or simply listening at home, on the road, or abroad.

ABOUT THE ARCHIVE
Recitations are reviewed before they appear in the catalog, so you hear quality, intentional recordings — not an unfiltered upload dump.
